# 02: The 3D board at 60 fps on the Galaxy A33

Status: in progress

The A33, the slowest device and the profiling target, drew the moving 3D board at about
60 fps on average with 52 to 60 at the 1% low. 60 at the minimum is the ideal, not a hard
requirement (the user, 2026-11-13). Task 042 has the profile: the lit shader over half the
frame's GPU cycles, its shadow's 9 taps about 15%, 4× MSAA 20 to 25%.

## Done (2026-11-13)

1. **The shadow in 4 taps on a phone** (`SHADOW_FEW`, `gui::phone`): 0.7 M cycles a frame
   saved, 7%. A tablet and a desktop keep the 9 taps: the 3 × 3 box's edges crawled in
   motion on the Mac ([decision](../docs/decisions/rendering.md)).
2. **fp16 in the lit shader**: blocked. Goose's `embed_shader` drops `mediump` (no
   `RelaxedPrecision` in the SPIR-V; [findings](../docs/goose-findings.md)). Once Goose
   emits it: `mediump` for the colors, harmonics, BRDF terms, tonemap, and the varyings
   that allow it; positions, shadow coordinates, and the frame map's uv stay `highp`;
   checked by shots on the A33, as the Mac's Metal translation keeps fp32.
3. **2× MSAA as a fallback** (`render3d::FEW_SAMPLES`): `src/native/display.c`'s timing first
   drops the pass to 2× and asks for 60 Hz only if 60 more frames are still slow
   ([decision](../docs/decisions/rendering.md)). 2× saves 1.4 M cycles a frame, 15%. The
   A33 takes it; the Pixel 7a doesn't.

After steps 1 and 3, `--bench 1000`, 611 MHz in every waited phase:

| Build | Waited P50 | P99 | Cycles | Presented avg | 1% low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main before | 15.58 to 16.15 ms | 17.74 to 20.92 | 9.52 to 9.87 M | 67.4 to 69.1 fps | 61.0 to 61.1 |
| After | 14.97 to 15.55 | 17.03 to 17.44 | 9.15 to 9.50 M | 74.8 to 75.1 | 63.8 to 64.0 |

The compositor's count (`tools/perf/device_perf.sh`): 70.6 → 75.1 fps on average, frames
shown for two refreshes 27% → 18.7%. Not slower on the Mac or the Pixel 7a.

Later, if not enough: the pieces' shadows analytic with the frame's shadow baked into the
plan, which drops the shadow map's pass (first in `docs/decisions/rendering.md`'s untried
list).

## How to measure

- The A33 holds the Play app: install builds beside it with `scripts/android.py
  src/main/draughts.goose --run --id-suffix NAME`, never uninstall it; uninstall the
  throwaway variants at the end.
- `--board 3d --screen game --theme wood --bench 300` (1000 for final numbers), the
  `summary` lines from `adb logcat -s goose`, started with `adb shell am start -S -n
  PKG/io.github.kaitk.draughts.DraughtsActivity --es args "'...'"`, HOME pressed first.
- The governor holds the GPU at 500 to 700 MHz whatever the work, so compare cycles:
  sample `/sys/kernel/gpu/gpu_clock` (kHz) every 1.5 s, waited ms × the mean clock;
  alternate builds, each at least twice, thermal status 0 at each run.
