# 02: The library's title over bright bark

Status: done

Centred in a tablet's landscape (`503e27a`), the puzzle library's "Puzzles" / "Ülesanded"
sits partly over the white birch bark of autumn's photograph, its first letter hard to
read (store scene 04, Play tablet, 2026-11-09). A soft shadow under the title, as the
player's chip got (`prototypes/chip.html`), or another way the app already uses.

The chip got a glass pill (take d), not a shadow; a pill would bring a new shape here, so
the title got takes b and e of the same prototype instead: `gui::shadowed`, the title
drawn over eight dark copies 2 dp round it at 20% and one 2 dp down at 35%, through the
text shader's own color (no shader change). A first try, the ring 3 dp out at 10%, left
spring's bark too bright round the letters. Headless shots before and after: only the
title's pixels change (autumn and spring at 1280 × 800, autumn at the iPad 13"'s
2752 × 2064, English and Estonian). The shell test's library draws 63 more glyph quads
(167 to 230).
