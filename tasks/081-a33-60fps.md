# 081: The 3D board at 60 fps on the Galaxy A33

Status: in progress

The A33, the slowest device and the profiling target, draws the moving 3D board at about
60 fps on average but drops to 52 to 60 at the 1% low. 60 fps at the minimum is the ideal
to strive for, not a hard requirement (the user, 2026-11-13). Task 042's "Where the A33's
frame goes" has the profile this task starts from: the lit shader (`LIT_FRAG`) is more
than half of the frame's GPU cycles, its shadow's 9 taps about 15% of the frame, and 4×
MSAA 20 to 25% (free on the Pixel 7a).

The plan, agreed with the user (2026-11-13), in order:

1. **The shadow in 4 taps.** `shadow()` in `src/render3d/shaders.goose` filters a 5 × 5
   texel box in 9 hardware-compared taps (Castaño's filter). The 3 × 3 box of the same
   filter takes 4 taps: about half the shadow's cost, slightly harder edges, which the
   pieces' size on a phone hides. Every device or only small screens: decide by the
   shots (the Mac at the Pixel's and the tablet's sizes, `docs/development.md`).
2. **fp16 in the lit shader.** `mediump` for the colors, the harmonics (`sh_eval`), the
   BRDF's terms, the tonemap, and the varyings that allow it; positions, the shadow's
   coordinates, and the frame map's uv stay `highp`. The Mali-G68 runs fp16 at twice
   the rate, and the shader held 64 registers on the tablet's G615, half occupancy (task
   059). No shader uses 16-bit yet. Shots against before: within task 065's margins.
3. **2× MSAA as a fallback.** On a device whose ready 3D board still misses its budget,
   the pass drops from 4× to 2× samples (`render3d_gpu`'s `SAMPLES`, `set_aa`, which
   the A key already switches the pass's way with). It decides as `src/app/display.c` decides to
   ask for 60 Hz: once enough frames of the ready board average over budget. Recorded in
   `docs/decisions/rendering.md`, which says gfx keeps 4× MSAA, and in
   `docs/architecture/`. The user moved the board at 2× on the A33 and found it fine.

Later, if these aren't enough: the pieces' shadows analytic (a disc's shadow stretched
along the light) with the frame's shadow on the field baked into the plan, which drops
the shadow map's pass too; ranked first in `docs/decisions/rendering.md`'s untried list.

## How to measure

- The A33 holds the Play app (upload-key signed): install builds beside it with
  `scripts/android.py src/app/draughts.goose --run --id-suffix NAME`, never uninstall it.
  Throwaway variants each get their own suffix; uninstall those at the end.
- `--board 3d --screen game --theme wood --bench 300` (1000 for the final numbers), the
  bench's `shadow map: cpu` and `summary` lines from `adb logcat -s goose`. Run from
  `adb shell am start -S -n PKG/io.github.kaitk.draughts.DraughtsActivity --es args
  "'...'"`, HOME pressed first.
- The governor holds the GPU at 500 to 700 MHz of its 897 whatever the work, so
  milliseconds swing between runs. Sample `/sys/kernel/gpu/gpu_clock` (kHz) every 1.5 s
  during the bench and compare cycles, waited ms × the mean clock; alternate the builds
  and run each at least twice. Thermal status (`dumpsys thermalservice`) 0 at each run.
- The baseline (main, 2026-11-13, 4× MSAA): 11.1 to 12.6 M cycles a frame, waited P50
  16.2 to 18.2 / P99 19.1 to 21.3 ms, presented 1% low 55 to 60 fps.

## Measured

Each step against the build before it, both beside the Play app (`--id-suffix`), alternated,
`--bench 300`, autumn's wood, thermal status 0 before and after every run. Cycles are
waited P50 × the GPU clock sampled through the waited phases.

**1. The shadow in 4 taps** (2026-11-13). The governor held 611 MHz through every run's
waited phases:

| Build | Waited P50 | P99 | Pass | Cycles | Presented 1% low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main (9 taps) | 16.10 to 16.15 ms | 18.44 to 18.72 | 14.4 to 14.5 | 9.84 to 9.87 M | 59.9 to 60.4 fps |
| 4 taps | 15.00 to 15.02 | 18.18 to 18.34 | 13.6 to 13.7 | 9.16 to 9.18 M | 61.4 to 63.0 |

About 0.7 M cycles a frame, 7%, near half of the 9 taps' 1.5 to 2 M. Shots on the Mac
(headless, a fixed clock) at the Pixel's 1080 × 2400 at 540 dp in both orientations and
the tablet's 1920 × 1200 both ways, wood and stone: mean difference 0.03 to 0.05 of 255,
all of it along the shadows' edges (at most 42 of 255 on 6,000 to 8,000 pixels of 2.6 M),
which look the same side by side even on the tablet's large pieces. So every device takes
the 4 taps: one shader, no switch by screen size.

**2. fp16 in the lit shader** (2026-11-13): not done. Goose's `embed_shader` parses
`mediump` and drops it: the SPIR-V has no `RelaxedPrecision`, so the driver never sees
it, and `precision` statements and `float16_t` don't compile
([findings](../docs/goose-findings.md)). The step waits for Goose to emit
`RelaxedPrecision`; then the qualifiers go in as step 2 says, checked by screenshots on
the A33, as the Mac's Metal translation keeps fp32.

**3. 2× MSAA as a fallback** (2026-11-13). `src/app/display.c`'s timing, the 60 Hz rule's,
now first drops the pass to 2× (`render3d::FEW_SAMPLES`) and asks for 60 Hz only if 60
more frames at 2× are still slow ([decision](../docs/decisions/rendering.md)). The bench's
waited phases come before the timing can decide (they are left out of it), so two builds
against the 4 taps: the fallback itself (`fb`), and 2× from the start (`two`) for the
waited cycles:

| Build | Clock | Waited P50 | P99 | Cycles | Presented 1% low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 taps, 4× | 611 MHz | 15.39 to 15.57 ms | 21.0 to 22.0 | 9.40 to 9.51 M | 61.3 to 64.4 fps |
| The fallback | 611 | 15.59 to 15.62 | 18.6 to 20.0 | 9.53 to 9.54 M | 58.4 to 58.5 |
| 2× from the start | 507, 611 | 15.83, 13.18 | 19.1, 17.1 | 8.03 to 8.05 M | 65.2, 70.9 |

The A33 takes the fallback in both runs, 60 frames into the presented phase (`3D board:
too slow for the display, 2x MSAA` in the log); its 1% low counts the frame that makes
the pass's targets again at 2×, once a session. 2× saves 1.4 M cycles a frame, 15%; the
governor gave one run 507 MHz for it, the same milliseconds for fewer cycles.

**After steps 1 and 3** (2026-11-13), `--bench 1000`, main before the task against the
fallback's build, two runs each, alternated, 611 MHz in every waited phase:

| Build | Waited P50 | P99 | Cycles | Presented avg | 1% low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main | 15.58 to 16.15 ms | 17.74 to 20.92 | 9.52 to 9.87 M | 67.4 to 69.1 fps | 61.0 to 61.1 |
| 4 taps and the fallback | 14.97 to 15.55 | 17.03 to 17.44 | 9.15 to 9.50 M | 74.8 to 75.1 | 63.8 to 64.0 |

The waited phases are still at 4× (the fallback decides only in the presented phase), so
their P99 shows the 4 taps alone; the presented frames are at 2× after the first 60. Over
60 fps at the 1% low in every run, against the baseline's 55 to 60 of task 042's runs and
61 of these.

**The Mac** (`--bench 300` headless at 2560 × 1600, before and after step 1, alternated):
the pass waited 1.65 to 1.66 ms before, 1.60 to 1.61 after; the frame with the shadow map
1.64 to 1.67 against 1.63 to 1.69. Not slower; step 3 is Android only.

**The iPhone 13 mini** (2026-11-13, the build holding both renderers): the 3D game on gfx
and on ngfx looked fine to the user.

**The Pixel 7a** (2026-11-13, `--bench 300`, main before the task against the final
build, two runs each, alternated, thermal status 0): the governor moved the G710 between
251 and 351 MHz, so cycles: 3.08 to 3.23 M a frame before, 3.23 to 3.26 M after, the same;
the pass alone waited 12.2 to 12.3 ms before, 11.8 to 12.0 after. It keeps 4× MSAA (no
fallback line in either run): its 60 Hz display shows the board's 60 frames one refresh each.

**The compositor's count on the A33** (2026-11-13, `scripts/device_perf.sh`'s 3D half run
against the test builds, 1000 frames, main before the task against the final build, two
runs each, alternated): on average 70.6 to 70.7 → 75.1 fps, frames on screen for two
refreshes 27.2 to 27.3% → 18.7%; the 1% low 45 in both, as any frame two refreshes long
sets it at 90 Hz. The README's row and the architecture's release table take these (the
release of 2026-11-10 had 60 / 45).
