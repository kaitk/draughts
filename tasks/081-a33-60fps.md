# 081: The 3D board at 60 fps on the Galaxy A33

Status: todo

The A33, the slowest device and the profiling target, draws the moving 3D board at about
60 fps on average but drops to 52 to 60 at the 1% low. 60 fps at the minimum is the ideal
to strive for, not a hard requirement (the user, 2026-11-13). Task 042's "Where the A33's
frame goes" has the profile this task starts from: the lit shader (`LIT_FRAG`) is more
than half of the frame's GPU cycles, its shadow's 9 taps about 15% of the frame, and 4×
MSAA 20 to 25% (free on the Pixel 7a).

The plan, agreed with the user (2026-11-13), in order:

1. **The shadow in 4 taps.** `shadow()` in `src/render3d/shaders.goose` filters a 5 × 5
   texel box in 9 hardware-compared taps (Castaño's filter). The 3 × 3 box of the same
   filter takes 4 taps: about half the shadow's cost, slightly harder edges, which the
   pieces' size on a phone hides. Every device or only small screens: decide by the
   shots (the Mac at the Pixel's and the tablet's sizes, `docs/development.md`).
2. **fp16 in the lit shader.** `mediump` for the colors, the harmonics (`sh_eval`), the
   BRDF's terms, the tonemap, and the varyings that allow it; positions, the shadow's
   coordinates, and the frame map's uv stay `highp`. The Mali-G68 runs fp16 at twice
   the rate, and the shader held 64 registers on the tablet's G615, half occupancy (task
   059). No shader uses 16-bit yet. Shots against before: within task 065's margins.
3. **2× MSAA as a fallback.** On a device whose ready 3D board still misses its budget,
   the pass drops from 4× to 2× samples (`render3d_gpu`'s `SAMPLES`, `set_aa`, which
   the A key already switches the pass's way with). It decides as `src/app/display.c` decides to
   ask for 60 Hz: once enough frames of the ready board average over budget. Recorded in
   `docs/decisions/rendering.md`, which says gfx keeps 4× MSAA, and in
   `docs/architecture/`. The user moved the board at 2× on the A33 and found it fine.

Later, if these aren't enough: the pieces' shadows analytic (a disc's shadow stretched
along the light) with the frame's shadow on the field baked into the plan, which drops
the shadow map's pass too; ranked first in `docs/decisions/rendering.md`'s untried list.

## How to measure

- The A33 holds the Play app (upload-key signed): install builds beside it with
  `scripts/android.py src/app/draughts.goose --run --id-suffix NAME`, never uninstall it.
  Throwaway variants each get their own suffix; uninstall those at the end.
- `--board 3d --screen game --theme wood --bench 300` (1000 for the final numbers), the
  bench's `shadow map: cpu` and `summary` lines from `adb logcat -s goose`. Run from
  `adb shell am start -S -n PKG/io.github.kaitk.draughts.DraughtsActivity --es args
  "'...'"`, HOME pressed first.
- The governor holds the GPU at 500 to 700 MHz of its 897 whatever the work, so
  milliseconds swing between runs. Sample `/sys/kernel/gpu/gpu_clock` (kHz) every 1.5 s
  during the bench and compare cycles, waited ms × the mean clock; alternate the builds
  and run each at least twice. Thermal status (`dumpsys thermalservice`) 0 at each run.
- The baseline (main, 2026-11-13, 4× MSAA): 11.1 to 12.6 M cycles a frame, waited P50
  16.2 to 18.2 / P99 19.1 to 21.3 ms, presented 1% low 55 to 60 fps.
