Draughts prototypes

Home A: board first

The game in progress is the home; labelled buttons beside it: continue, a friend, the computer with its level, puzzles with stars.

Home B: cards

Three solid cards on the table, each with a little scene made of pieces, a title, and a line.

Home C: title menu (chosen)

The season's photograph and a 3D board, with the wordmark, the language, settings, and a menu of words on a glass column.

Home, icon tiles

The first take: three glass tiles with icons only.

Settings

Reached from the title menu: the background (by the date or one season), the board (2D or 3D), move hints, and the language, as rows of large tiles.

Game

A selected piece and its capture, a king that must capture, and the side to move over undo, hint, restart, and back (below the board in portrait).

Puzzle picker

Three theme rows of ten (capture, become a king, the only safe move), with 0 to 3 stars.

Puzzle solved

Stars over the dimmed board, again and next.

Boards

The four season boards in 2D and 3D, crowned and stacked kings, and 10×10.

Fonts

Tenor Sans, Libertinus Sans, Belleza, Marcellus, with Estonian text.

The switcher at the bottom right changes season, board theme, view, king style, background (the photograph, blurred behind the 2D board), move hints, and font on any page.
