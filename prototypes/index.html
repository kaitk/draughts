Draughts prototypes

Home

The season's photograph and a 3D board, with the wordmark and a menu of words on a glass column: continue, a friend, the computer, puzzles, settings.

A new game

A friend or the computer opens its setup in the column: which draughts, the level, the clock, then play.

First start

Which draughts (international or Russian), learn the moves or just play, the tutorial's steps with the moves shown, and what to play first.

Settings

Reached from home and the game: the background (by the date or one season), the board (2D or 3D), move hints, and the language, as rows of large tiles.

Game

The board between the way out (home, settings, start over) and the play (whose turn, take back); with clocks.

Puzzle picker

Three theme rows of ten (capture, become a king, the only safe move), with 0 to 3 stars.

Puzzle takes

Playing one puzzle in the game's layout: the goal, the stars at stake, a wrong move sliding back. a, a hint tile and the solved card; b, the goal drawn as the pieces to take, a hint only after a miss, the theme's ten with next; c, no hint tile (the piece glows after two misses) and next in the panel. The switcher's take and state (play, wrong, hint, solved) change it.

Puzzle solved

Stars over the dimmed board, again and next.

Win takes

A won game, about 2 s and then still, without the drifting sparks. a, the winner's pieces answer with a ripple of glow and the winning piece lifts; b, a warm light blooms behind the card and a few sparks rise from behind it; c, the winning piece glides from its square to the card and takes its crown. The switcher's take changes it; click to play it again.

Exit takes

A way to close the app from home. a, a quiet Exit row under the menu; b, a round X in the top right corner; c, a door in the same corner. The switcher's take and language change it.

Gear takes

Home's settings beside the wordmark in portrait. now, the quiet 88 dp tile; a, the gear alone in its 88 dp tap; b, the X's round glass button. The switcher's take and language change it.

Boards

The four season boards in 2D and 3D, crowned and stacked kings, and 10×10.

The switcher at the bottom right changes season, board theme, view, king style, background (the photograph, blurred behind the 2D board), and move hints on any page.
