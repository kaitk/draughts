Draughts prototypes

Home

The season's photograph and a 3D board, with the wordmark and a menu of words on a glass column: continue, a friend, the computer, puzzles, settings.

A new game

A friend or the computer opens its setup in the column: which draughts, the level, the clock, then play.

First start

Which draughts (international or Russian), learn the moves or just play, the tutorial's steps with the moves shown, and what to play first.

Settings

Reached from home and the game: the background (by the date or one season), the board (2D or 3D), move hints, and the language, as rows of large tiles.

Plain background takes

A soft fade in place of the season's photograph, matched to the season's board (task 044). In settings: a, a Picture row of its own (photo or plain); b, a two-part switch under the Background row's name; c, one Plain tile at the row's end. The four seasons' fades behind home, the flat game, and the 3D game. The switcher's place, plain, grad (a, the 3D backdrop's grade of the page color; b, darker and calmer), and language change it.

Game

The board between the way out (home, settings, start over) and the play (whose turn, take back); with clocks.

Lookout and hint takes

Help in a game (task 040), round 3, built as take a with drain and the shaded square: two controls, the lookout (a toggle that while on shows the danger by itself) and the hint (the piece, then where to, a pip a hint). a, the lookout a round toggle on whose turn's line; b, a tile among the way-out ones. The danger dark and still on the pieces (shade, drain, or shadow), the hint light and breathing. Every mark and state, both controls and the names. The switcher's options (folded under Options in portrait) change it.

Game around the 3D board

The game's UI round a WebGL board with the native camera (task 015). now, the current layout; steep, the camera 65° down; top, the board pushed up; corners, the panels in the top corners beside the narrower far edge; bleed, the frame running off the screen. The switcher's take changes it; a drag turns the board.

Captured pieces takes

What each side has taken (task 052), at the tablet, a phone, and the smallest iPad window, flat and raised. a, in the left side (the left panel, under the bar's tiles, the compact bar's middle); b, on the table at the board's left. Each as the app's pieces in a row, stacked discs, or a count, early or late in a game. The header's cap, look, state, flat, and season change it.

Puzzle set takes

Choosing the draughts (International, Brazilian, Russian) on the puzzles' library, and the library reworked (task 037). now, today's library with no choice; a, a three-way switch in the title row over today's rows; b, the draughts' name under the title opening a small picker, with a Continue row, stars and what's next per theme, and locked themes as a path; c, a grid of theme cards with Continue first and the new game's draughts tiles in the title row. The switcher's take, view (library or home, its Puzzles count following), draughts, tie (shared with settings or the puzzles' own), Brazilian (shown or hidden), stars, the picker open, and language change it.

Puzzle takes

Playing one puzzle in the game's layout: the goal, the stars at stake, a wrong move sliding back. a, a hint tile and the solved card; b, the goal drawn as the pieces to take, a hint only after a miss, the theme's ten with next; c, no hint tile (the piece glows after two misses) and next in the panel. The switcher's take and state (play, wrong, hint, solved) change it.

Player takes

A player per child on one tablet, each with an animal, their own look, stars, level, and game (task 038). a, the player's face beside the name opens who's playing over home; b, who's playing on every launch; c, a row of faces on home, one tap to switch. The switcher's take, state (home, pick, add, edit, hold), players, and language change it.

Games' history takes

The moves of the game with their marks (best, ?!, ?, ??), the games played with a rating, and the Moves setting (task 074). On the game screen: a, a panel under the play panel; b, under what each side has taken; c, the play panel grown to hold the list; portrait a strip of the last moves. The games screen, a game replayed, the Moves setting, and where the games screen is reached from (home or the player's sheet). The switcher's view, take, marks, state, entry, place, and language change it.

Player badges

Five styles for a player's picture (task 038): carved pieces like home's king, nature tokens, animal lines, coloured rims with an initial, silhouettes at dusk; then where the badge shows on home, four ways, and the first start's pick.

Boards

The four season boards in 2D and 3D, crowned and stacked kings, and 10×10.

The switcher at the bottom right changes season, board theme, view, king style, background (the photograph, blurred behind the 2D board), move hints, and the plain background (plain, grad) on any page.
