# Design boards prompt

A prompt for an image model (ChatGPT) to make design boards: PNG mood boards and screen
options that the HTML prototypes then settle. Paste it whole; ask for one board
per reply, varying the direction named at the end.

---

Make a design board (one 1920×1200 PNG, landscape) for a draughts learning app for
children of about 5 to 8, played on a 11-inch Android tablet (1920×1200, used in landscape
and portrait). The game is Russian draughts: 8×8 board, the
pieces on the dark squares, kings that fly along diagonals. Later a 10×10 international
board.

What the app is: play against a friend on the same tablet, play the computer at three
levels, and about 30 puzzles in three tiers ("find the capture", "multi-jumps and
crowning", "the only safe move"). Controls during a game: undo, hint, restart, back.
A celebration when a game is won or a puzzle solved.

Who uses it: younger children who can't read yet and older ones who read a little. So:
icons first, very large touch targets (at least 64 px on screen), legal moves always
shown, no text needed to play. Calm and warm, not loud or cartoonish: no mascots, no
neon, no ads, no coins or shop.

The look:
- Dark frosted glass panels over a calm scene, like a premium weather or music app:
  panels about 40% tinted card colour over the scene dimmed to 60% brightness with a
  little extra saturation, thin light 1 px borders, soft highlights on the top edge,
  rounded corners. Flat buttons on the glass, no bevels, no gradients on buttons.
- The scene behind the glass is a soft, out-of-focus picture (for example a Nordic
  landscape in one season: winter snow, spring birch forest, summer sea, autumn
  leaves), blurred heavily.
- The board and pieces are simple, crisp shapes: discs with a rim and an inner ring,
  soft shadows, no photographic textures except as an option. Kings must be obvious at
  a glance (a crown mark or a stacked double disc).
- Show the game states on the board: one selected piece lifted with a soft glow, its
  legal target squares marked with glowing dots, and a piece that must capture
  pulsing with a ring.
- Typography: a humanist sans with flared strokes, in the style of Optima (for example
  Libertinus Sans, Tenor Sans, Marcellus or Belleza). All text in English, and one line of the Estonian
  translation ("Kabe — mängi sõbraga, õpi ülesannetest") to show õ ä ö ü.

On the board, show:
1. The home screen: three large glass tiles, "Play a friend" (two figures), "Play the
   computer" (a small robot or chip icon with three level dots), "Puzzles" (a puzzle
   piece), with the scene behind.
2. The game screen in landscape: the board filling the height on the left, a glass side
   panel on the right with undo, hint, restart and back as big icon buttons, and whose
   turn it is shown by a piece colour, not by text.
3. The same game screen in portrait (a smaller inset), with the controls below the
   board.
4. The puzzle picker: three tiers as rows of rounded tiles with 0 to 3 stars each.
5. A swatch strip: the board's light and dark square colours, the two piece colours,
   the glow colour, the glass tint, and the text colour, each labelled with its hex
   value.

Direction for this board: [pick one: "wood": warm light and dark wood board, cream and
dark-walnut pieces, autumn scene | "stone": pale and slate stone board, white marble
and black basalt pieces, winter scene | "flat": flat muted colours, sage and deep green
squares, ivory and terracotta pieces, summer sea scene].

Lay it out as a clean presentation board on a neutral dark background, with small
captions under each item. No device frames, no watermarks, no extra decorations.
