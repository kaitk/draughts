The skeleton and density

  • Elbows turned up and down in the limb's own plane, then back: with gravity on, the limbs step and run level, nearer the photos.
  • The limbs leave the trunk at Sapling's down angle (Weber-Penn), flatter than before.
  • Twigs hanging from under level wood (Weber-Penn's negative AttractionUp, as Sapling's willow).
  • The crown from the room as full as before step one (shedding 0.3, three short shoots a twig, the nudge apart on).
  • Limbs crooked only at their elbows; the photos' limbs bend all along.
  • The hanging twigs read as curtains; low flat limbs are short, cut by the crown's outline; the plates a little clumpy.
  • Growth now about 78 ms on the PC in all (the nudge apart 28 ms of it), near the 100 ms budget. Without the nudge, 24 ms for growth.
  • The table loses its sun patch in this view: the fuller crown shades the window.

The bark's material (after the user's specification)

  • --tree-detail helped most: fine cork relief and small cracks, the plates' edges ragged; the furrows stay dark.
  • --tree-albedo (warm grey-brown to olive grey, weathered tops lighter) and --tree-lichen help a little, close up the most.
  • --tree-spec: a weak broad highlight, roughness 0.55 on shoots to 0.9 on the old trunk; a dry sheen, not waxy. The sky's reflection was left out (it made the trunk hazy).
  • Scattering by age showed nothing visible from the room; left off, as the specification allows. Round one's 0.5 everywhere looked fleshy against the light.
  • Bark textures 57 ms once at startup (34.6 before); +0.013 ms a frame on the high tier.
