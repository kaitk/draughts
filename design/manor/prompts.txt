Manor concept prompts (task 093/01). Images stay local here (.gitignore).

The concept, fixed for now (2026-11-17):
- style-01.png: the library, the upper floor's middle room on the garden side; warm
  afternoon sun from the left. The wood board.
- upper-hall-02.png: the hall at the top of the stairs, glazed doors onto the balcony over
  the entrance portico; morning sun. The stone board.
- garden-facade-01.png: the house from the park, trees within metres of the windows.

Each room gets two framings, as the app would show them:
- Menu: the home screen. The window is in focus and the picture; the board waits on its
  table at the side, soft and out of focus.
- Play: the camera where a player's eyes would be, the board and table in focus, the
  room soft behind.

What works with ChatGPT: few nouns and a mood. It draws every noun it is given, each on
its own, so long lists of details fail; it redraws rather than edits, so "the same room"
in a new chat gives a new room. Attach the concept image instead of describing it.
Gemini may keep an attached room closer: try both. One prompt per new chat, the concept
image attached fresh each time. Earlier rounds' prompts are in git history.

Save results as <room>-<framing>-NN.png: library-menu-01.png, library-table-01.png,
library-desk-01.png, hall-menu-01.png, hall-table-01.png.
