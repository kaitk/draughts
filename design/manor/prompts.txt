Each room gets two framings, as the app would show them:
- Menu: the home screen. The window is in focus and the picture; the board waits on its
  table at the side, soft and out of focus.
- Play: the camera where a player's eyes would be, the board and table in focus, the
  room soft behind.

What works with ChatGPT: few nouns and a mood. It draws every noun it is given, each on
its own, so long lists of details fail; it redraws rather than edits, so "the same room"
in a new chat gives a new room. Attach the concept image instead of describing it.
Gemini may keep an attached room closer: try both. One prompt per new chat, the concept
image attached fresh each time. Earlier rounds' prompts are in git history.

--- 30. One room in isometric, a game's view, from words only. ---
The room in a clean isometric view from above, like a room in a game: the whole floor and all four walls in view, the two near walls cut away at floor level so nothing stands on them. The library of a Baltic German manor house in Estonia, about 1850: a room of about 7 by 9 metres on the upper floor, in the middle of the garden front. Three tall windows alike on one long wall, evenly spaced, in deep niches. Behind the opposite long wall is the hall at the top of the stairs; other rooms lie beyond the end walls. A tall tiled stove in a corner, fed from outside the room. Two or three doors where such a house would have them, none squeezed in.
